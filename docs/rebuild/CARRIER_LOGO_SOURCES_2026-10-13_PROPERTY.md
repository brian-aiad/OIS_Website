# Property market logo sources — October 13, 2026

Eight previously text-only property entries now have actual logo artwork retrieved from official company sites. Existing Foremost artwork was retained. These are source-verification records, not a claim that a separate trademark license or agency brand approval was granted. Carrier qualifications and the supplied relationship list remain unchanged.

Files are in `original-insurance/src/assets/carriers/`. Official SVGs were rasterized with Sharp without redrawing or changing logo colors. Raster images were proportionally resized where needed; no generated/reconstructed logo artwork was used.
